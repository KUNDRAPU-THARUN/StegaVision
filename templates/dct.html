Concept notes

What is DCT Steganography?

  • Images can be divided into 8×8 pixel blocks.
  • The Discrete Cosine Transform represents spatial image information as frequency components.
  • Selected coefficients can be modified to carry message bits.
  • This implementation uses mid-frequency coefficients and avoids the DC coefficient.

DCT-based embedding is an educational technique, not a guarantee of secrecy or resistance to image processing.
